DuduAdmin / Platform administration

Manage organization provisioning and platform operations.

For platform administrators provisioning organizations, managing resources and reviewing operational summaries and activity logs. Organization staff and channels are managed in Console.

Usage and setup documentation ↗Explore the support tour ↗

Key capabilities

Organization provisioning

Provision organizations and their initial administrators.

Organization management

Find organization records, manage status and inspect organization details.

Resource overview

Review organization resource summaries and operational overviews.

Activity records

Review administrative activity records for routine checks.

How it works

  1. Sign in as a platform administrator.
  2. Provision an organization and set its initial administrator.
  3. Organization administrators configure the team in Console; agents handle conversations in Desk.

Requirements and scope

Platform administration is separate from organization management. Admin does not handle agent conversations or replace channel account applications and authorization.

The modules work together: Admin provisions organizations, Console configures teams and channels, Desk handles conversations, and Server provides shared services. MockPulse is an optional development tool.
